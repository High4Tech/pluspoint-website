## Content Boundaries

- The original pages conflict on experience, workforce and project totals. Do not combine these into new metrics.
- Certification, ISO, 100% compliance and guaranteed turnaround claims have not been verified and are not reproduced as guarantees.
- No client testimonials were found. The quote section uses the actual published leadership message, explicitly attributed as leadership, not a client endorsement. Approved client quotes remain an outstanding content item.
- Named project details are concise summaries of the original listing. Images are explicitly illustrative or company-gallery material, not verified evidence for those projects. No fabricated dates, client names, crew counts or outcomes.
- Portfolio photographs reuse URLs visible on the original portfolio plus existing local assets. The corporate stage is generated concept imagery. See `DESIGN-CONTINUITY.md` for asset rights and public-launch gates.
- New copy is edited for readability with authored Arabic translations. It is not a headless WordPress integration.
- The enquiry remains a reviewed email draft. It does not submit to a server or claim that a message was sent.
